# Thumbnail & Title Options

---

## Title Options

### Hook-First (Best CTR for science/tech audience)

1. **"He Asked 'What Just Happened?' — Then Built the Open-Source Answer"**
   - Curiosity gap + narrative hook
   - Works for both science and tech audiences

2. **"The Open-Source AlphaFold3 Is Here. Here's Why It Matters."**
   - Direct, clear value proposition
   - Good for people searching for AlphaFold3

3. **"Google Closed AlphaFold3. One Scientist Built It Open."**
   - Conflict-driven, David vs Goliath framing
   - Strong emotional hook

### Technical / Search-Optimized

4. **"OpenFold3 Explained: Open-Source Protein Structure Prediction"**
   - Clear for search, good for long-tail SEO
   - Less exciting but findable

5. **"I Predicted a Protein Structure in 3 Commands (OpenFold3)"**
   - Personal, demo-forward
   - Good if the channel is personality-driven

6. **"OpenFold3 vs AlphaFold3: What's the Difference?"**
   - Comparison format, high search intent
   - People searching for AF3 will find this

### Bold / Provocative

7. **"AlphaFold3 Should Have Been Open Source"**
   - Opinion-driven, encourages clicks and comments
   - Polarizing (good for engagement)

8. **"The $600 Million Problem With AlphaFold3"**
   - Money hook + controversy
   - References Isomorphic Labs funding

## Thumbnail Concepts

### Concept A: "The Quote"

```
[LEFT SIDE]                    [RIGHT SIDE]
Photo of AlQuraishi            Protein structure
or silhouette                  (blue/red pLDDT coloring)

[OVERLAY TEXT]
"What just happened?"
```

- Clean, minimal, curiosity-driven
- The quote in large white text is the hook
- Works with titles 1, 3

### Concept B: "Open vs Closed"

```
[LEFT SIDE]                    [RIGHT SIDE]
AlphaFold3 logo               OpenFold3 logo
with LOCK icon                 with UNLOCK icon
(red tint)                     (green tint)

[OVERLAY TEXT]
"OPEN SOURCE"
```

- Visual contrast, clear story
- Lock/unlock is immediately readable at small sizes
- Works with titles 2, 3, 6, 7

### Concept C: "The Structure"

```
[FULL FRAME]
Beautiful protein structure
(blue confidence coloring, dark background)

[OVERLAY TEXT]
"3 Commands"
or
"Open Source"
```

- Visual-first, relies on the beauty of the protein
- Works if the render is stunning (ChimeraX with good lighting)
- Works with titles 2, 4, 5

### Concept D: "The Demo"

```
[FULL FRAME]
Terminal screenshot with
`run_openfold predict` visible
+ protein structure floating above it

[OVERLAY TEXT]
"It's Free"
or
"Try It Now"
```

- Targets developers and researchers
- The terminal signals "you can do this"
- Works with titles 4, 5

### Concept E: "David vs Goliath"

```
[LEFT SIDE]                    [RIGHT SIDE]
Google/DeepMind logo           Columbia University
(large, dominant)              (small, scrappy)

[CENTER]
VS

[OVERLAY TEXT]
"Open Source Wins"
```

- Conflict framing, strong for CTR
- Works with titles 3, 7, 8

## Thumbnail Design Rules

- **Text:** Maximum 4-5 words, readable at mobile size (120x90px)
- **Faces:** Thumbnails with faces get 30%+ higher CTR — use AlQuraishi's photo or your own face cam still if possible
- **Colors:** High contrast. Blue protein on dark background pops. Red/green for conflict framing.
- **Avoid:** Small text, cluttered layouts, generic stock photos
- **Test:** View at 120x90px before finalizing — if you can't read it, simplify

### A/B Testing
If your platform supports it, test title #1 vs #3 — one is narrative-driven, the other is search-driven. They'll attract different audiences and tell you what your channel's viewers prefer.
