## Description (Copy-Paste Ready)

```
In 2018, Mohammed AlQuraishi watched DeepMind solve protein folding and asked, "What just happened?" When AlphaFold3 went closed-source, he built the open alternative. This is the story of OpenFold3 — a fully open-source reproduction of AlphaFold3 for predicting protein, DNA, RNA, and drug structures.

In this video:
- The 50-year protein folding problem and why it matters
- How AlphaFold changed biology (and won a Nobel Prize)
- Why AlphaFold3 going closed-source was controversial
- OpenFold3: what it is, how it works, and why it matters
- Live demo: predicting a protein structure in 3 commands
- How diffusion models generate 3D structures from noise
- The open-source structure prediction landscape (Boltz, Chai, ESMFold)
```
